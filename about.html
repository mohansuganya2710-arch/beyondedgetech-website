About us

Land is a long decision. We keep the paperwork short.

We develop approved residential layouts with laid roads, drainage and street lights, and hand over plots with documents that a bank will accept.

Approved layouts

DTCP and local body approval with a clear title, patta and encumbrance certificate shared before booking.

Ready infrastructure

Tar or concrete roads, storm drains, water lines and electric poles are in place at handover.

Loan support

We work with banks that already know our layouts, so plot loans move faster.

Visit before you decide

Our team walks you through the boundary stones, road width and your plot's facing on site.

What you receive

Documents with every plot

Ask for copies at the site office before you book. Nothing is hidden behind a payment.

  • Sale deed and parent documents
  • Patta and approved layout copy
  • Encumbrance certificate
  • Property tax and layout development receipts
  • Agreement of sale on booking
