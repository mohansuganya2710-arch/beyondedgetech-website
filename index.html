How buying works

From first visit to registration in five steps

  1. Choose a layout

    Compare projects, plot sizes and prices on the Projects pages.

  2. Visit the site

    Book a slot and see the plot, road and surroundings in person.

  3. Verify documents

    Check the approval, patta, title deed and encumbrance certificate.

  4. Book with an advance

    Pay the booking amount and receive an agreement of sale.

  5. Register the plot

    Complete the loan, pay the balance and register the sale deed.

Good to know

Common questions

Which documents do I receive?

You receive the sale deed, patta, approved layout copy, encumbrance certificate and the tax receipts. You can check them before booking.

How big is one cent?

One cent is 435.6 square feet, and 100 cents make one acre. A 1,200 sq.ft plot is about 2.75 cents.

Can I get a bank loan for a plot?

Yes. Banks lend for approved layouts. Use the loan calculator to estimate your EMI, and our team helps with the documents your bank asks for.

Is there a charge for the site visit?

No. Book a slot on the Site Visit Booking page and we will confirm it on WhatsApp.
